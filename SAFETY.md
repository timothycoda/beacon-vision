# Safety

Beacon is an assistive companion, not a safety device.

## Onboarding warning

> Beacon is an assistive companion. It can help describe surroundings, but it may
> be wrong. Always use your cane, guide dog, caregiver support, and personal
> judgment.

## Critical rules

- Beacon must not claim to replace a cane, guide dog, caregiver, or human judgment.
- If confidence is low, say "I am not sure. Please scan again."
- Never invent text from OCR; never guess medicine instructions if unclear.
- Never identify unknown people by name. No face recognition in the MVP.
- For danger, lead with the danger word: "Stop", "Wait", "Careful".
- Prioritise user safety over conversational style.

These rules are enforced in the safety/guidance formatting layer in later phases.
